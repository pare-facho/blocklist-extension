## Summary (Chrome: 132 characters max)
Block the far right: hides the posts of listed far-right accounts, whether you follow them or not. X for now.

## Long description

Pare-Facho hides on X the posts of far-right accounts, based on lists you choose. It does not touch your account: no block, no report, nothing is sent to X.

What it does:

• Hide. Posts from listed accounts disappear from your timeline, replies, search and profiles as they render. Reposts made by a listed account are hidden too. Nothing changes on X, only your screen.

• A ⛔ button on every post: one click to hide and add the account to your list. Five seconds to undo.

• Your lists, your control. The list of the "On bloque l'extrême droite" website is subscribed by default and refreshed automatically; you can remove it, subscribe to other addresses, add your own accounts, and "keep" any account so it is never hidden.

• Settings page: accounts hidden, subscribed lists, full list with search, activity log.

Privacy: no data collected, no server. Everything stays in your browser. The only connections are the list downloads (the default one and those you add).

Be aware: hiding only works in this browser; the mobile app shows everything. When X changes its layout, the extension may stop hiding until it is updated.

Free software (GPL-3.0). Source code: https://github.com/pare-facho/blocklist-extension

## Permission justification (Chrome)
- storage: save lists, settings and activity log, locally.
- alarms: re-download the lists periodically.
- Host access to x.com: hide posts from listed accounts in the page and show the ⛔ button. No request is sent to X.
- Host access to pare-facho.github.io: download the default list and its updates.
- Optional host access to other sites (optional host permission https://*/*): requested only, and case by case, when the user adds a list address, to download it.
- No remote code: all code is in the package.
