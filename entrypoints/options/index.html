Blocking

Posts from listed accounts are always hidden. Real blocks are optional and run slowly in the background, only while an x.com tab is open and you are logged in.

Automated actions are against the terms of most networks. Slow pacing lowers the risk for your account; it does not remove it. Use a secondary account if you can.
